[Your Name]
[Your Address]
[City, State, ZIP]
[Date]

[Recipient's Name]
[Recipient's Title]
[Company/Organization Name]
[Company/Organization Address]
[City, State, ZIP]

Dear [Recipient's Name],

I am writing to inform you that, due to illness, I will be unable to attend work from [start date] to [end date]. I am currently under medical care and am expected to make a full recovery.

I apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and will make sure to catch up on any missed work as soon as I am able to return to the office. I will provide a doctor's note or any other necessary documentation to support my absence.

I would like to request sick leave for the above-mentioned period. I have [number] days of sick leave available, and I would like to use [number] days to cover my absence.

Please let me know if there are any issues or concerns with my request. I appreciate your understanding and support during this time.

Thank you for your attention to this matter.

Sincerely,

[Your Signature]
[Your Name]
**Supporting Documents:**

* Doctor's note or medical certificate
* Any other relevant medical documentation

**Note:**

* Please modify the application to fit your specific needs and circumstances.
* Be sure to include the necessary details, such as the dates of your absence, the reason for your absence, and any supporting documentation.
* Keep a professional tone and format throughout the application.
